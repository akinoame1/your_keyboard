# The layout

Prose is a phone keyboard for writing long prose. Top to bottom:
- The echo line: the text around the cursor, shown large, so what is being typed can be read without looking up. Tapping it moves the cursor; it can be dragged to sit above or between the rows (echo.placement). Buttons at its right end: paste, undo after a paste or clear, the field's own send button where it has one, and ⋮ for actions.
- Optional rows above the letters: a digit row (keys.numberRow) and a one-click row of the symbols used most (keys.symbolRow, with its symbols in symbols.oneClick, or per app in symbols.oneClickPerApp).
- Three rows of letters from the alphabet being typed (alphabets, the ones switched on in languages). Every key is the same width, as wide as the widest row allows; a shorter row's spare width goes to its ends as the alphabet's align says. The bottom letter row starts with shift (where the alphabet has one) and ends with backspace, in the same place in every alphabet.
- The bottom row: ?123 (the symbol layer), a comma, the space bar, a full stop, enter. Swiping across the space bar changes alphabet, through the ones switched on.
- The symbol layer (symbols.pages) replaces the letters with symbols in the letters' own places, on the Latin grid, in every alphabet; a key in the shift's place turns the page. Page one is also what the Latin letters type on a long press.

What chat cannot change yet: what a key or gesture does, the bottom row, the echo line's buttons, colours and the theme. For those, reply {} and say what would be needed.
