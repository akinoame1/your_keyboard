# Typing helpers

Typing helpers: a space after , . : ; ? ! and a spaced dash (text.autoSpace); a capital at a sentence start (text.autoCapitalise); each press moved to where the thumb aimed (keys.aimCorrection).

Each is one setting, on by default:

- keys.aimCorrection (true/false): move presses to where the thumb aimed.
- text.autoCapitalise (true/false): shift by itself at a sentence start.
- text.autoSpace (true/false): a space after , . : ; ? ! and a spaced dash.
