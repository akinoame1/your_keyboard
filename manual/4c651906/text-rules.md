# Typing helpers

Typing helpers: a space after , . : ; ? ! and a spaced dash (text.autoSpace), a capital at a sentence start (text.autoCapitalise), and an aim correction that moves a press to where this person's thumb meant to land (keys.aimCorrection).

Each is one setting, on by default:

- keys.aimCorrection: correct presses toward where the thumb aims — true or false.
- text.autoCapitalise: shift by itself at a sentence start — true or false.
- text.autoSpace: a space after , . : ; ? ! and a spaced dash — true or false.
